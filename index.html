What you can do with FlowSync.

Roadmap, sprints, issues, GitHub activity and deployments in one simple tool.

Plan your work

Turn goals into releases, and releases into sprints your team can actually finish.

  • Roadmap & releasesPlan versions with target dates and see how close each release is to done.
  • EpicsGroup stories, features, tasks and bugs into bigger goals, with progress for each.
  • SprintsRun two-week sprints with goals, a backlog and a clear view of what's done and what's left.
  • Team capacitySet each person's hours and days off per sprint, and see who is overloaded before the sprint starts.

Track every piece of work

Every issue type, one board, and time tracked against the plan.

  • All issue typesStories, features, tasks, improvements, bugs and subtasks, each with priority, due date and hour estimate.
  • Kanban boardDrag and drop cards through a workflow that only allows the right moves.
  • Time loggingLog time against estimates and see when work runs over.
  • Bulk actionsReassign, reprioritise or reschedule many issues in one step.

Work together

Conversations, files and history live right on the issue.

  • Rich comments@mentions, file attachments and a full change history on every issue.
  • NotificationsAn in-app notification bell, with optional sounds for assignments, mentions, comments and urgent issues.
  • "My work" pageEach person's assigned and watched issues, all in one place.

Connect code & releases

See the code behind every issue, and the release that shipped it.

  • GitHub linkingBranches, commits and pull requests that mention an issue appear on it automatically.
  • Deployment recordsLog releases by hand or from your build pipeline (CI), and see what is running in each environment.

Lead with confidence

Spot risks early and keep every sprint on track.

  • Manager overviewTeam workload, plus overdue, stuck, unassigned and waiting-for-QA work at a glance.
  • Personal dashboardEach person's day, current sprint and the latest activity.

Built for teams & companies

A private, secure workspace that fits the way your company works.

  • Private workspaceEvery company gets its own workspace, and each company's data is kept separate.
  • Clear rolesOwners, admins, managers, developers, QA and guests, each with the right access.
  • Easy onboardingInvite people with a single expiring link, and organise them into teams and projects.
  • Make it yoursAdd your company logo and profile pictures, and choose from Light, Dark, Pink, Sunset and Ocean themes.
